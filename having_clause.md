## ASSIGNMENT ON HAVING CLAUSE

1. WAQTD DEPARTMENT NUMBER AND TOTAL SALARY NEEDED TO PAY ALL EMPLOYEES IF THERE ARE AT LEAST 4 EMPLOYEES IN EACH DEPARTMENT.
```SQL
SELECT DEPTNO, SUM(SAL) "TOTAL SALARY OF ALL EMPLOYEES"  
FROM EMP
GROUP BY DEPTNO
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 4;

-- OUTPUT
    DEPTNO TOTAL SALARY OF ALL EMPLOYEES
---------- -----------------------------
        30                          9400
        20                         10875
```
---
2. WAQTD NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES EARNING SALARY MORE THAN 1200 IN EACH JOB, AND THE TOTAL SALARY NEEDED TO PAY THEM MUST EXCEED 3800.
```SQL
SELECT JOB, COUNT(*) "NO OF EMPs WITH SALARY > 1200", SUM(SAL) "TOTAL SALARY OF JOB"
FROM EMP
WHERE SAL > 1200
GROUP BY JOB
HAVING SUM(SAL) > 3800;

-- OUTPUT
JOB       NO OF EMPs WITH SALARY > 1200 TOTAL SALARY OF JOB
--------- ----------------------------- -------------------
SALESMAN                              4                5600
PRESIDENT                             1                5000
MANAGER                               3                8275
ANALYST                               2                6000
```

```SQL

FROM EMP
```

8. WAQTD MINIMUM AND MAXIMUM SALARIES OF EACH JOB IF MINIMUM SALARY IS MORE THAN 1000 AND MAXIMUM SALARY IS LESS THAN 5000.
```SQL
SELECT JOB, MIN(SAL) "MIN SALARY OF JOB", MAX(SAL) "MAXIMUM SALARY OF JOB"
FROM EMP
GROUP BY JOB
```
